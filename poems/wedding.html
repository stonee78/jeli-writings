Wedding

What is it that makes two people join?
Is it duty, tradition, or a verdict from the flip of a coin?
Is it meant to be?
(The sparks ignite when they meet.)
What about Soulmates?
(What they feel is like standing at heavens gates.)
Is it Friends of the best kind?
(Their life together good and bad they wouldn't dare rewind.)
I believe its destiny.
(I know they feel it and we all can see it, they are made for each other perfectly!)

Daniel & Heather's Wedding 2011 · 2011
