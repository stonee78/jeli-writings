Goodbye

So long to what has been a run.
Race for the ending,
End dawnings come.
We live on in flesh,
Though eternity is the soul.
Farewell, Godbless,
To you I never told.
Happy Birthday is newly meant.
A goodbye so sorrowful,
But the memories are well kept.
My wait is unknown to us.
Soon I will be at peace,
Understanding God's love,
Then everything will be at ease.
I leave you with my prayers
And keep my head up till my end.
When I leave all these haters
And have life once again.

2009
