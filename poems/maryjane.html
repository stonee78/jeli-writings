Maryjane

To Mary: I miss you.
But I'm coping without you.
So tedious was your smoke
Like tenticles reaching for my throat.
Your smell like beautiful roses
But sick were your thoughts
And all selfdoubting poses.
I saw myself in a world of denials.
The mere epidemy of unruly depression
Over and over you were the cause for my mistrials.
Unforgettable are the memories of your resin.

2001
