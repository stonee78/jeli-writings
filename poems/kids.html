Kids

My kids know where they stay.
Even if it's late, at another place,
they know how to pray.
Some say don't spank them,
place in a corner and hope for a better day.
That's how Supernanny got her show.
From parents who have no balls,
are just blowin smoke.
You've got to be consistent, hold your ground.
When something goes wrong,
they don't need explanations of what's going down!
You think in a situation when you tell them "MOVE!" they'll know the danger?
They say "NO! WHY?! I DONT WANT TO!" next thing you know, they're in the possession of a stranger!
But there's a fine line between discipline and losing your mind.
If they get on your nerves, suck it up and put them aside,
anything more than a spanking is an unforgivable crime.
Parents understand! You lead by example. Wondering why their doin drugs?
You think it's cause they smelled weed, out in the streets? Instead of hugs, getting mean mugs?
Don't wait till it's too late, they only get one childhood.
You Fuck this up, they'll never forget, then your old thinking it's all good.
But back to the beginning, children are a part of you.
As a kid; with a friend and a toy we could always figure out what to do.
We didn't understand the world we lived in.
But we knew love & hate & needed forgiveness when we sinned.
So remember the innocence and put yourself in their shoes.
All they ask for is love and security. If all else fails, give them that and you can never lose!
