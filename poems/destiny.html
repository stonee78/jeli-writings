Destiny

Chased to the end.
Something so simple causes reality to bend.
I find myself in the middle of the end, that just began.
How do u fix something made to be broken.
Like a family tree, you see the lines but never next to kin.
Take my lies, take my deceit.
If I were me, straight to hell to feel the heat.
Unless the stars is where I reach.
But with two of us it feels like lead on my feet.
If it was obvious could I admit it?
Staring at promises in an empty pit.
Maybe written in stone where I can see,
but over my eyes is the veil of destiny.
Dreams of death and despair.
There in the back, a glimpse of hope, with a shiny glare.
I can't give up, I won't give in!
Passion to erupt, happiness without sin!

2011
