New

This I found, is it new? Good night, spelled right,
I'm ready for another day, I remember, this prayer.
It was answered but was it forever?
Pulled to my past,
A rush that I once felt came fast.
I'm not fooled, this isn't it.
I know the outcome, like I put together a kit.
Yet I yearn for it. A tender kiss.
Something I miss.
Did I find this?
But at what price.
Its paid, I've paid.
Dearly.
Were these answers to my prayers? It was never said.
Can u hear me?
