My Life

My life. Picture a string of humiliation,
a ball of hate, an ocean of anger.
Reassuring the end.
How much longer can I take?
You have no meaning,
like words written on a cake.
Remember the times scared and alone?
The only time I needed you,
you beckoned to postpone.

2000

Author’s note, as printed in Woven Magic
Jesus E. Lopez, Corpus Christi, TX
This poem is dedicated to my best friend, Raul Ramirez III. He passed away August 17, 2000. This poem depicts the misery and pain inflicted to the heart and soul from people and things around us. But even in the worst pain, there is always peace in the end.

First published in Woven Magic, The International Library of Poetry (poetry.com), 2001. ISBN 1-58235-557-6.
