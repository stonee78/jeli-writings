In a Hole

Some say I'm in a whole, can you dig it?
My heads in the clouds, maybe I'm fly, can you feel it?
Even if you fall in the gutter,
It's got to go somewhere.
I'll make it, and when I get there,
I could be something you and I could share.
Like they say, what goes around comes around.
Maybe you;ll have what I didn't,
someone to help you up when you get pulled down.
