Take a step to 10 yrs.
Where do you see us?
If you knew, would you have fear?
Memories of you and the girls are what ill have most.
Though us together 20 yrs is what I'll boast.

Do you see the chimes in the wind making their notes.
We're at our 20th anniversary making a toast.
Here's to all the good times and bad.
Cause without them our marriage we would not have.
Our girls. The best memories are to come.
When you hold your grandchild. Our babysitting days are not done.

Will we grow old, sweeter like wine.
Of course everything changes with time.
But if we stick through these turns in our daily grind,
the happiness was always there, not hard to find.

You with grey hair, me with a beard,
Thank God we made it, being old and lonely, I've always feared.

Now gone, when I'm lowered I see you disappear.
Here's a tissue, thought I told you for me shed no tears.
I'm sorry I left you, God said, the decisions' not mine.
But my love is still alive, it's not bound by life, nor death or time.
Just know I'll always be with you, I've said these things before.
I know what your thinking, but my dear, we can't argue anymore!
