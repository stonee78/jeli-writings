Through My Eyes

Through my eyes only
Will you see the road,
The anger, the journey, the pain.
For the sake of being free from
The jealousy around me.
Through the times I struggled in vain.
Look in through my eyes.
Did you ever think life would be like this?
One world, one life, one love.
Was the thought His?
But what do they think
Keeps us so called sane.
One of many trying, not most,
To stay out of the rain.
