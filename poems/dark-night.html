Dark Night

Content note: this poem speaks about suicide. If you are struggling, you can call or text 988 (U.S. Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) at any time.

Don't want this night to ever end. Think of the times, clock ticking, video's playing, fighting the sleepy bend of my eyes. I don't want tomorrow to come. I feel like suicide. Like ending the story on chapter 13. Must I write the rest of my life? Can't I dream it, in the eyes of forever? It was intended for us to suffer. It feels hopeless. Given a gift, help me use it oh Lord. I crave the dwelling of sorrow. Comforting in it's loathing belly. This must be wrong, otherwise it will be my undoing. Slowly I seem to strive, backtracking in my will to survive.

2003
