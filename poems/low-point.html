Low Point

Down, low, hurt, under the rug.
How else can I explain me feeling like a slug.
In a world that seems to change seemingly is very real
But to my surprise this change is just what I feel.
This life is what you make of it, why is there a flaw?
Though were made in his image, perfect we never are.
It's like a caged match in my mind, the winner is still trapped.
Only to find a new fight once the loser is adapt.
Can this get any easier, for it's taking the life right out of me.
Just keep my head above water is what I must do to survive this beat.

So many variables, like an algorithm in the making,
It may be rocket science but I must learn before I'm forsaken.
If not I, then no one will rescue me.
Wait, I'm lying, Jesus will always be there for me.
Its hard to trust in people, which makes my faith stronger.
Who else to put your trust in than an all knowing perfect Father.
This is my faith and it has been proven time again.
Something intangible that can only be experienced first hand.
This I cannot explain to people, but pray for them I must.
If their undoing is not knowing then I hope my poems will help them trust.

In God, we strive to be, but not with malice, revenge or impurities.
But with an open heart to love all as He did for humanity.
I hope I'm revealed the beauty of the universe, galaxies, planets and black holes.
For the secrets of the universe and why we are, is the answer most of us want to know.
I leave u with this in mind, when questioned if there is a God, why for some its hard to believe.
Don't think literally, open your mind.
Like Einstein said, "The more we learn about the universe the more it becomes apparent what a higher power has achieved."

2010
