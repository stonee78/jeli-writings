Young One

Young one. Waiting to get over life.
When will it be better,
Follow all advice, how to overcome strife,
Be grown, an adult with kids and a wife.
What you don't know is the struggle never dies.
Young pain is new.
Obscuring your view. Now,
Very familiar pain as a reminder.
At this age isn't life meant to be kinder?
One can lose their mind dealing with the daily grind.
Callus & pessimism take over without thought.
You must remember Love, Faith, Patience is what Jesus taught.
Understand letting go. Although it may seem so,
Misery causing events, You have no control.
Follow the light because in the portrait of life,
We are a Grand Plan revealed through Gods' might.

2013
