Angry Mess

An angry mess is what's left of it.
I've tried and tried again to rid myself of this pain but it shifted.
To what do I owe this weight that's been chained to me.
Seems the older I get the more I can just live with it.
I can't describe it really but if I saw it i'd Know.
It's RED eyes, Ugly Head, with wings of a Crow.
To try and catch it is like pausing time.
That remote never works and yet I keep trying.
It's a simple demon to remove but within it there lies,
Everything I stand for and most of my drive.
You see there can be no evil if there is no good.
Light wouldn't shine if darkness never could.
As I read this back I see this picture I've realized,
Tomorrow hasn't come yet and today was such a surprise.
Once more Ink has touched paper, the chains are shifting I sense.
That beast with wings of a crow has left it's layer; let the healing commence.

2006
