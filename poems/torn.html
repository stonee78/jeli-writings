Torn

Sometimes I feel my thoughts are loud
and can be overheard.
But not to my surprise, when they are
I am never understood.
Could it be, I have an ecstasy
for loneliness and a saddend sound?
Or the fact remains that I'm in chains
and the key cannot be found.
With my stare my torment deepens,
it seems I cannot repair,
all the scars I've found inside
and more I fear are there.
But with no spite I cease my fright.
Forgiveness can I spare?
Because if not mine, who will then find
forgiveness for me to share.
Why must these cycles be turned,
for it's something we'd have learned.
But He said our choice is in our nature.
Well then? "JUDGEMENT" Court's Adjourned.
