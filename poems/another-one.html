Another One

Sail on. Like a summer breeze.
I need a happy thought because
all my writings are dark with a little sleaze.
I picture wide open skies and the land full of trees.
Country living puts life in perspective.
The only place I can truly feel free.
But the big city calls me at times.
With the skyscrapers and tons of lights.
Ridin in a leather laced sports car cause all you wanna do is shine.
I don't know. My thoughts seem contrived.
I know who I am and where I'm going.
By Faith I survived,
and have never feared not knowing.
