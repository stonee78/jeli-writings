Enough

I thought it was enough.
The hard work, determination and drive.
But I failed like a fish out of water trying to stay alive.
Tossed aside like leftover soup.
All my efforts meant nothing, like a gov't after a coo.
Why didn't I see this coming, were the teachers more enlightened than me?
Or did I not reach high enough, I shot for the clouds and all I got was the tree.
Compared to a fool, how could I not see it?
Tears in my eyes, this must be what it feels like to wanna quit.

But I will do my time, punishment in this prison I've set.
Now released, how could I ever believe "defeated" is what I would get?
With a new mission in mind, sights set to the stars while I sing a new tune.
This lesson I've learned I took it to heart, now here we go to the next level, straight to the moon!

2011
