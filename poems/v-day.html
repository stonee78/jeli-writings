V Day

Happy Valentine's Day.
To my life, my wife, my lover, my friend.
Some things I've gotta say.
Number 10. Is how many V days we've shared.
Although not all good, we would not be here if I didn't care.
Thank you for sticking around.
All this hard work I've done, I promise not to let you down.
Don't forget I'm only human.
So for mistakes I've made, forgiveness can u lend?
