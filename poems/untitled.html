Untitled

Undermined by it all. There is a higher purpose. Transitions in life fall. Though my faith brings me justice.

Can one feel your own future, as dim as it seems we all trust. Lured into the pit forced to endure, my so called life in my hands turned to mush.

When will I see or rather live. Listen to the scriptures and start to learn. A love higher than us that always gives. Now can you feel that empty yearn?

For just the little we take for granted, and the faith that we must hold, can bring life to the sick and saddened and the hearts of the poor pure as gold.

So listen to my lines and read between the wisdoms, these words alone are not mine. to his people it is God that gives them.

2003
