Time

Life, work, school, failure, success, young, old, live and die.
All we do there is one constant; time.
So regardless of your venture, it does not matter how long it takes.
Time does not discriminate, time passes. It does not wait.
What is now will be then. Everyone is measured by the same time.
What will you do with yours?

2010
