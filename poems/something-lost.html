Something Lost

This love was innocent, willing to give,
You gave just as much, but ruled by fear.
In the middle of flight, you chose not to live.
This was the first time I emanate an intimate tear.
This is my classroom! I passed with a cold heart.
And only one last time would I not understand
It happened again, a fourth of my heart wud depart.
Considered my loss, my life laid out in your hand.
I lived in solitude a world cold and lonely
If you saw me smile, myself would call me phony.
My heart so sandy, desolate and reprieved,
That one step in, you were destined to retreat.
I needed someone compassionate,
and who else could I call,
Someone just as scarred and distressed,
Comforting each other, and at the rest withdraw.
You were something I once felt before,
An object you could not be,
The other half of my heart restored,
Love feels but does not see.

The future isn’t bright
Like fairy tales would have you believe.
But love always prevails,
Didn’t I tell you? You’re a little naïve.
