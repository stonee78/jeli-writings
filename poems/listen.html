Listen

Two eyes, yes their open but I can't see two steps in front of me.
Two ears and I can hear but do I listen to what is being said to me?
In the midst of it all, I can't seem to sort it. Can you feel the desperation as it fills My chest,
Cause with out you I can't decode this not even at my best.

If we could all see, how these affect each one of me,
What can it all mean;
The lazy don't survive, only the lean.
Now I will leave this be.
Is there a happiness that was never foretold to me?
Not in a dream, a song or even a mention from thee?
I never lie and wait for it to show,
Because I know in life, if you don't start it then you'll never get to go.
Here's to you, the one who brought me through.
How can I forget, the perfect blend, the resemblance, I would be a fool.

2010
