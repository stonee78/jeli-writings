Jesse

As paper soaks ink again,
Schools of memories and
Disoriented pains surface again.
But the new has taken its space.
To think everything has an end,
Just the beginning looking at the face
Into teary eyes of confused pain.
Windows to the soul they say,
But who can see this cloudy entity
Of the person you call Jesse.

2002
