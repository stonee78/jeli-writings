Never Scared

Never scared you could say.
But a lie is a lie
even when there's truth inside.
Wake up from darkness is what follows when one opens' their eyes.
A cloud shrouds your vision.
Look closer, He's all smiles inside.
Did it kill him? Not hate, jealousy or death.
The one lesson learned, he fought it with every last breath.
What good is it to gain the world but lose your soul?
Borrowed from the Bible.
Don't ignore it, change your goal.
Dark & corruptible I once thought we were.
As judgement passed, a glimpse of Hell but with a scent of mir.
There is always a way back, it's not too late!
If you can read this, you still have time!
Cease the hate!
