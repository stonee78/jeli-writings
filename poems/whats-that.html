What's That

What's that?! The dog's barking again,
Unsettling fear, I must have a peep.
Quietly creepin' to get the upper hand,
As I glide to the window, intensely discreet.

Now glaring, my gleaming gaze is aroused.
Waiting for the slightest movement.
Should I get my gun... Wait! Just then I paused.
The dogs are now stagnate,
It was just Santa Clause.

2003
